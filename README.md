## A Square Basis Theorem for Matroids

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24135970.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24135970)

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.24135970](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24135970) · Preprint, 11 November 2026 · MSC 05B35, 13F65, 14M25

For every matroid M of rank at least one, the basis-exchange square relations contain a ℤ-basis of the integer kernel ker<sub>ℤ</sub> A<sub>M</sub> of the basis incidence matrix. Equivalently, over any field the toric ideal I<sub>M</sub> becomes, after inverting the variables, a complete intersection of codim I<sub>M</sub> symmetric exchange binomials. The proof is constructive and combinatorial and uses no computer calculation. Uniform matroids admit an explicit unitriangular square basis, and the theorem yields a Plücker-ratio criterion for when two real matrices differ by an invertible row transformation and an invertible column scaling.
