A Square Basis Theorem for Matroids

For every matroid of rank at least one, the basis-exchange square relations contain a ℤ‑basis of the integer kernel of the basis incidence matrix. Consequently the toric ideal of a matroid becomes a complete intersection of symmetric exchange binomials after inverting the variables. The proof is combinatorial and self-contained; uniform matroids admit an explicit unitriangular square basis, and the theorem yields a Plücker-ratio criterion for equivalence under row transformation and column scaling.
